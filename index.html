      Balance Sheet

      Assets

      • The main assets of the business will be the GPU stocks, e commerce website, payment system and other technical systems used for the business
      • The business will have the GPUs bought from overseas as the main stock assets
      • The website and other systems developed for the online store will also be part of the business assets

      Liabilities & capital

      • The business liabilities will include payments that have to be made for suppliers, payment gateway, SMS gateway and other business services
      • The remaining money after paying the business costs and liabilities will be part of the business capital

      Financial Ration

      • Gross profit ratio

        Gross profit ratio can be used to check the profit made from selling the GPUs after the cost of buying the GPUs

      • Net profit ratio

        Net profit ratio can be used to check how much profit the business gets after paying all the business expenses

      • Current ratio

        Current ratio can be used to check whether the business has enough current assets to pay the current liabilities

      • Return on investment

        Return on investment can be used to check how much return the business gets from the money invested to start the business

      These financial ratios can help to understand whether the business is making enough profit and managing the money properl
